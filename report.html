What happens next, and what is expected of you

  1. Step 1
    Go to the station to sign your statement

    Reporting online starts the process, but the statement has to be sworn and signed in person. Take your ID and your reference number.

  2. Step 2
    Keep your case number

    It is sent to you by SMS. You need it to check the case or to escalate it.

  3. Step 3
    Preserve the evidence

    Do not clean or repair the scene yet. Keep damaged items, messages and receipts, and upload photographs while things are as they were.

  4. Step 4
    Be available

    Answer the investigating officer, and attend identity parades and court when you are asked to.

  5. Step 5
    Report anything that changes

    New information, property recovered, or a new contact number - tell the investigating officer or update this case.

  6. Step 6
    Withdrawal is not only your decision

    You may ask to withdraw, and a station commander decides on the request. Once the docket is with the prosecutor, whether the case proceeds is the NPA's decision, not yours and not the police's.

Get medical attention as soon as you can Go to a hospital, clinic or a Thuthuzela Care Centre. If you can, do not wash or change your clothes first - that evidence cannot be recovered later, and it can decide a case.

What you are entitled to

From the Service Charter for Victims of Crime. These are rights, not favours, and you can insist on them.

  • Dignity and privacyTo be treated with courtesy, and to have your case handled privately.
  • To offer informationTo tell the police what you know, and to have it recorded.
  • To receive informationTo be told what is happening with your case, and why.
  • ProtectionTo be protected from intimidation for having reported.
  • AssistanceTo be referred for medical, counselling and support services.
  • Compensation and restitutionTo ask the court for compensation, or for your property back.
If you are not getting anywhere Escalate in this order - each step is recorded:
  1. The station commander - you can do this from your tracking page
  2. The National Complaints Centre on 0800 333 177
  3. IPID, the Independent Police Investigative Directorate, for misconduct by an officer
