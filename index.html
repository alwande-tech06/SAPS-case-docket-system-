South African Police Service

Report a crime from anywhere, and keep proof that you did.

You do not need an account, and you do not need to be at a police station. Your report is recorded and given a reference number the moment you submit it, before any police official sees it.

Report a crime Track a report I already made
Crime in progress or someone in danger? Call 10111 now. This form is for reporting incidents that have already happened.
  1. Step 1
    You submit a report

    Tell us what happened, where, and how to reach you. We send a code to confirm your number.

  2. Step 2
    You receive a reference number

    Keep it. It is your proof that the report exists, and it is issued before any police decision is taken.

  3. Step 3
    The report is routed to the nearest station

    Jurisdiction is decided by the system from the incident location, not by whoever you speak to.

  4. Step 4
    An official must record an outcome

    A docket is opened, a refusal is recorded with reasons, or the matter is referred. There is no fourth option, and none of these can be deleted.

  5. Step 5
    You are told if nothing happens

    If no case number is issued within 24 hours, the system notifies you and raises the matter with the station commander.

Other ways to report

  • In personAny police station. Ask for your report reference number before you leave.
  • By phoneThe service complaints call centre can capture a report on your behalf.
  • On your behalfA school, clinic or shelter may report for someone who cannot.

Your information

Your details are handled under the Protection of Personal Information Act. Only the officials handling your case can see them, and every time someone opens your case it is recorded.
