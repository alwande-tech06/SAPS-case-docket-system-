Administrator

User accounts

Accounts are deactivated, never deleted, so that every historical audit entry still resolves to a real person. An account cannot be deactivated while it holds open dockets or exhibits.

What an administrator cannot do There is no route, form or control anywhere in this system by which an audit entry can be edited or deleted, including from this account. Administration covers who may act, never what has already been recorded.
